# Envoy core v2: drives and keystones come back (design, 2026-11-10)

Design only. Nothing here was built or run. Branch `port/envoycore2`, worktree `C:/melee-wt/envoycore2`, off
`pub/rust-port` (`2926328f3`). By Fable, revising `full-game-plan.md` (port/fullplan) sections 4.2, 4.6, 5 and 7 and
`envoy-redesign.md` sections 2 and 3. Everything else in those two notes stands.

Tags: **[R]** read from source or a note (cited), **[I]** inferred, **[J]** judgement, **[U]** unverified,
**[D]** an owner decision already taken (memory `envoy-lab-answers-2026-11-09.md`), **[O]** the owner's own words.

## 0. One page

**Why this note exists.** The owner played milestone 1 ("Crests core", port/m1, `envoy-crests.md`) and said: "just all
around mediocre, the old envoy was better" [O]. Asked what he liked in the old Envoy, he answered all four [O]:

1. visible power in fights: drives firing often, with their effects on screen;
2. the drive models: seeing the actual drive objects;
3. building up: stacking drives and keystones, growing stronger over the run;
4. big, game-changing effects instead of +12% bumps.

Crests were built to be the opposite on each point: rare (they fire at moments, 2-5 times a fight), invisible in fights
(redesign Q7), flat (no tiers, six slots, one hone) and small (+20%, +35% in a 3 s window) [R `envoy-crests.md`,
`envoy-redesign.md` 2]. So the reward object changes back. The run around it does not.

**The design in ten lines.**

- The reward object is the **drive** again: a small 3D object (the owner's own `envoy_drives` models) with **one rule**.
- Equipped drives **orbit the fighter in play**. Each has a charge that fills from what you do (hits, landings,
  jumps, tags, time). A full drive **fires**: the model darts out, a large effect lands in its family colour, a Brawl
  sound plays, the drive goes dim and recharges.
- A second copy of a drive **levels it up** (1 to 3). There is no bag, no affixes and no dead duplicate.
- **Keystones** are one rule and one price. They change how the whole build plays. One is picked at the run's start,
  one after each of the first two bosses: three per run.
- Drives come from **floor drops in the fight** (walk over the model) and from a **pick of three** between fights.
- Effects are **big**: bursts that deal 6-20%, statuses that last seconds, an extra jump, a shield orb, every drive
  firing at once on a tag. A written budget keeps the screen readable (section 3).
- Everything that decides anything is in `envoy_sim`, hashed every frame. Effects and sounds are drawn by `envoy_ui`
  from the sim's fire log and cannot change the fight.
- Kept as decided [D]: 3 acts x (3 fights + boss) and the Short run; elite, Petey, Master Hand + Crazy Hand; the Brawl
  disc required, Brawl music and SFX anywhere; pool 5 co-op / 4 solo; the encounter kinds; the extra stages;
  content-only unlocks and NG+; saves at boundaries; Second Wind offline; team attack as a toggle; the in-place tag;
  the door and the camp; no old GD art in the functional UI.
- Reused from milestone 1: the data-driven pool, the lint, the full-state digest, the per-fighter wearer, the host
  imports. Dropped: the 26 crests, the moment families, Fresh and Benched, hones, "not drawn in fights".
- **Next milestone (V1): a one-week vertical slice.** A 5-fight solo run with 9 drives, 3 keystones, the models in orbit,
  fires with effects and sound, floor drops and levels. Its only purpose is the owner's verdict on the feel.

keystone allowance, the strength percentage, the Momentum counter, Guarded, Shock as a separate word.

## 2. The core loop

### 2.1 A drive

| field | values | notes |
|---|---|---|
| `family` | RED damage, GREEN speed, BLUE defence, YELLOW air, PURPLE status, WHITE wild | the six model bodies; shape carries the family as well as colour [R models README] |
| `rule` | one sentence, at most 12 words, at most 2 numbers | lint-checked (4.1) |
| `charge` | what fills it and how much is needed: hits landed, hits taken, landings, jumps, seconds, or an event (tag in, shift, KO dealt, stock lost) | one source per drive |
| `effect` | one effect from the sim's list (2.4) | |
| `number` | one i32 (x1000) | scaled by level |
| `level` | 1, 2 or 3; the number is x1.0 / x1.6 / x2.4 [J, for the harness] | level 3 also widens the effect where the rule says so |
| `gives` / `reads` | 0 or 1 word each from **Burning, Chilled, Marked, Haste** | the synergy edge, the same word in both texts |
| `wearer` | main or partner | the fighter that picked it up, or the one chosen on the card |
| `look`, `sound` | ids into the presentation tables | never read by the sim |

The four words: **Burning** (2% a second), **Chilled** (walk, dash and air speed -30%), **Marked** (takes +30%),
**Haste** (your walk, dash and air speed +30%). Burning and Marked exist in port/m1 already [R `envoy-crests.md`].

### 2.2 Earning drives

| source | when | rule |
|---|---|---|
| **floor drop** | the first KO you deal in each fight drops one; an elite drops two; a pack drops one per two foes [J] | the roll comes from `seed, fight, drop index`, so a retry and both peers agree. It lands on a floor point of the current stage, never where the foe died. It is a **zone in the sim**, not an item: items stay off. The fighter that touches it wears it, at once, mid-fight |
| **pick of three** | at every fight boundary (the existing reward hold) | three cards, each showing the model turning, the rule, and "new" or "level 2". The player picks the card, then the fighter. The `+1 stock` card replaces the third when the pool is at 1 (kept from port/m1) |
| **keystone pick** | run start; after the act 1 boss; after the act 2 boss | three keystones, each with its rule and its price |
| **collect hold** | after the last KO, up to 10 s while a drop is on the floor | the old hold, shortened; an arrow marks the drop |

Rules that make a dead reward impossible:

- A drop or card for a drive the fighter already wears **levels it up**. A level 3 drive is never offered again.
- A fighter with every slot full is only offered copies of what it wears; the other fighter gets the new kinds.
- Never two cards of the same drive; at least one card connects to a word the pair already gives or reads (the
  redesign's slot A rule, kept).
- A quit mid-fight resumes at the fight's start [D]: drops from that fight are rolled again, identically.

### 2.3 Slots, levels and the power curve

| | start | act 2 | act 3 | NG+ lap |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| drive slots per fighter | 3 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| keystones (per player, not per fighter) | 1 | 2 | 3 | +1 per lap |
| top level | 3 | 3 | 3 | 3 |

A full 12-encounter run gives about 9 picks and 10-14 drops: enough to fill 8 slots and take most of them to level 2
or 3 [I]. Growth is visible as more orbiters, ring overlays for level (one ring at 2, two at 3: the existing
`drive_ring_magic` and `drive_ring_rare` models) and larger effects. Foes scale by an integer budget from the pair's
total levels with the +25% cap [R strength decision 3.3], set at fight boundaries only. No strength number is shown.

### 2.4 Firing

1. Every equipped drive of the fighter in play has a charge counter in the sim. The benched fighter's drives hold
   their charge.
2. The sim sees the sources without new engine work: **hits** through the wired hook `damage.fighter_take_damage`
   (pre / replace / post) [R `modhost.md`]; **landings, jumps, dashes** through the wired `action.*.enter` rows [R];
   **tags, shifts, KOs, stock losses** as port/m1 already detects them [R `envoy-crests.md`].
3. A full drive fires on the next free frame. **One fire per fighter every 20 frames**: a second full drive waits,
   glowing, and fires next. This is what turns a pile of triggers into a rhythm.
4. A fire is a sim event: it applies its effect and appends `(frame, drive, wearer, target, x, y)` to an 8-entry
   fire log in the published view.

Effects available to the sim, by what exists:

| effect | how | status |
|---|---|---|
| damage burst (percent to one foe or to all in a radius) | `gd_fighter_set_percent`, positions from `gd_fighter_get_pos_*` | exists |
| status on a foe: Burning, Marked | port/m1's windows | exists on port/m1 |
| Chilled, Haste | `gd_envoy_apply` speed on the foe's or the wearer's slot | exists; the foe-slot write needs a check [U] |
| heal (active or benched) | `gd_envoy_percent` | exists on port/m1 |
| bonus damage on a hit (crits, "+30% to Marked") | extra percent in the take-damage post hook | exists |
| absorb the next hit's damage | `GD_SKIP` in the take-damage pre hook | exists; knockback still applies [U] |
| extra air jump | a fighter field write (`gd_fighter_write`) | [U], a one-day probe |
| **a blast that launches** | a one-frame hitbox on the engine's hit path, as Petey's attacks are [R full-game-plan 4.5]; new import `gd_envoy_blast(x, y, r, damage, angle, kb, team)` | **new, M**, Opus; V2 |
| intangible frames | `gd_fighter_set_intangible` | new, S (already planned) |

Attribution in co-op (which player's hit filled the charge) needs the victim's damage-source field [U]; solo play
does not need it. Drive effects never hit a teammate, whatever the team-attack toggle says: the toggle exists for
deliberate friendly hits [D Q11], and an automatic burst is not deliberate [J].

### 2.5 Keystones

One rule and one price from the fixed list of seven, each learned once: **Slow** (walk and dash -15%), **Low** (jump
-15%), **Fragile** (take +25%), **Weak** (deal -20%), **Vulnerable** (statuses on you last twice as long), **Lock** (tag
cooldown 10 s), **Bleed** (lose 1% whenever a drive fires). Never a status as a price. A keystone belongs to the
player and is active for whichever fighter is in play. It is permanent for the run. It is drawn as a **halo** over
the fighter's head (the `drive_ring_unique` crown model, tinted per keystone), so a glance at a fighter shows how
many keystones it carries.

### 2.6 How it meets the rest of the game

| system | rule |
|---|---|
| **in-place tag** [D] | Drives are per fighter. In the 60-frame cinematic the outgoing fighter's orbiters fly off and the incoming fighter's fly in. Tag-in is a charge source (Slipstream, Relay). The in-place prototype respawns the fighter and loses attribute writes [R `envoy-crests.md`]: the sim re-applies Haste and prices on the release frame |
| **co-op** | Each player has their own drives, keystones, picks and (recommended, Q8) their own drops. Both pick in one freeze with their own pads (kept). Orbit radius differs per player so two fighters standing together stay readable |
| **shared pool 5 / 4** [D] | Unchanged. `+1 stock` stays a card. Last Light keys on a stock lost |
| **stage shifts** | Surge keys on the shift. Zones (drops) move with the same "nearest floor" rule fighters use [R full-game-plan 3.3] |
| **elite** | Carries one or two drives, shown orbiting and on its card (Q7) |
| **Petey** | His HP is separate from percent [R 4.5]: the boss framework gains `damage(amount)` so bursts and Burning count. Chilled and Marked act at half strength on any boss. A caged partner's drives are dark |
| **Master Hand + Crazy Hand** | Stamina HP through percent [R 4.5]: bursts apply as they are [U] |
| **Second Wind** (offline [D]) | A rewind restores sim state, so charges, levels and drops rewind with the fight |
| **saves at boundaries** [D] | The record holds drive id, level and wearer, and the keystones. Charges and drops are not saved |
| **unlocks, NG+** [D content only] | 12 drives and 3 keystones are open at the start; the rest unlock by clears and NG+ levels. A lap keeps the build and adds a keystone |
| **encounter kinds, stages, door, camp** [D] | Unchanged. The camp shows the pair's drives as the models |

### 2.7 Online and rollback

- The sim owns drive ids, levels, wearers, charges, the fire queue, statuses, zones, offers and keystones. All of it
  is in the one `#[repr(C)]` state struct that port/m1 already folds into `gd_hash_word` with FNV-1a every frame,
  with the plan and `POOL_DIGEST` [R `envoy-crests.md`]. A divergence in any charge shows on that frame.
- Hook callbacks run inside the sim, so they re-run in a resimulation. Random rolls use the sim's seeded generator.
- `envoy_ui` draws from the fire log. A fire is keyed by `(frame, drive, wearer)`. If a rollback removes a key, its
  effect fades in 4 frames; if the key returns it is not replayed.
- Sounds start from the same log. The Brawl play calls are fire-and-forget and always answer 0, so the sim cannot
  branch on them [R `melee_modhost/src/lib.rs`, the `gd_brawl_*` imports]. The sim never calls them.
- The pool digest is in the handshake token as planned, so two peers with different drive pools refuse each other at
  the camp.
- Synctest target for V1: depth 4 over 3600 frames across fires, pickups, tags and picks, 0 mismatches (the port/m1
  script's shape).

## 3. Seeing it: models, effects, sound and the budget

### 3.1 The drive models

- The bodies, the glass shell and the rings are reused as they are: they are the owner's own generated models, and
  his rule against old GD art covers the functional UI, not these [D correction 2026-11-09]. `make_drives.py` can
  generate more bodies per family if 20 drives need more than 6 silhouettes (Q for a later step, not now).
- **In orbit:** up to 4 around the fighter in play, at chest height, about a fifth of the fighter's height each. Glow
  follows charge: dark body when empty, bright seams when full (the atlas already has that ramp [R models README]).
- **On a fire:** the model leaves the orbit, travels to where the effect lands in 6 frames, flashes, and returns dim.
- **On the floor:** the model inside its glass shell with a beam, as in the old game.
- **On cards and in the camp:** the model turning slowly, in place of an icon.
- **New port work:** nothing in the Rust port draws a `.gxmesh` today, and UI mods cannot draw in the world (no
  projection import) [R grep of `rust/`, the `gd_ui_*` list]. V1 adds a small host renderer for these meshes and a
  presentation import `gd_ui_world_model(model, x, y, z, yaw, scale, glow)`, depth-tested, read-only on the sim. If
  that is not working by day 3 of V1, the slice ships pre-rendered sprites of the same models, billboarded, and the
  mesh path lands in V2.

### 3.2 Effects

Each drive has its own shape; the family gives the colour. V1 draws them with projected additive quads and SDF
shapes from `envoy_ui`; the `melee_gx` effects system replaces them in V5 through the gallery, three candidates a
round [D E28]. Statuses are a surface tint on the foe; overlapping tints are fine [O audit ruling].

### 3.3 The budget

| limit | number | why |
|---|---|---|
| orbiters | 4 per fighter in play; 2 on an elite or boss; so 8 + 2 in co-op | "three loud looks is about what a player can name at once" [R visual identity 4.1]; 4 is the slot count |
| fires | one per fighter per 20 frames; at most 6 on screen a second | a rhythm, never a pile (2.4) |
| one fire | at most 30 frames, then gone; nothing persistent except statuses and orbiters | "loudness inversely proportional to duration" [R visual identity 0] |
| screen-wide effects | one at a time, keystone or level 3 only, at most 30 frames | [R visual identity 4.1] |
| text during a fight | none on a fire, a pickup or a level-up. The level-up is the ring appearing and a sound | the audit's rulings [O] |
| link flash | when a drive's effect reads a word another drive gave (Shatter on a Chilled foe), a short line joins the two models | ruled "awesome" [O] |
| target fire rate | about one fire every 2-3 s with three level 1 drives; about one a second with a full build | a harness band; the owner sets the dial (Q5) |

### 3.4 Brawl sound and music

Allowed anywhere, read at runtime from the player's own disc, never shipped [D Q10]. `gd_brawl_sfx` and
`gd_brawl_music` exist on rust-port [R `brawl-audio.md`]; only streams and wave sounds play (sequenced sounds do
not). Uses: one sound per family for a fire, a pickup sound, a level-up sound, a keystone fanfare, the boss stream
for Petey (built). The actual sounds are picked with the owner from an audition list, a few per step.

## 7. Risks

| risk | effect | answer |
|---|---|---|
| the mesh draw path slips | the slice has no models, which is one of the four things asked for | the sprite fallback on day 3 |
| automatic bursts knock foes out of the owner's combos | "big effects" that a Melee player resents | V1 bursts deal damage only; Q6 decides which drives may launch |
| the screen gets noisy again | the October complaint returns | the budget of 3.3 is enforced in the sim (the 20-frame rule) and by the lint's fire-rate band, not left to taste |
| co-op attribution field unverified | the wrong player's drive charges | solo first; a one-day probe in V2 |
| per-frame hook cost under rollback | resim time | charges are integer adds; the V2 perf baseline measures it |
| balance by CPU proxy | the CPU lands hits at a different rate than a person, so fire rates are off | the owner's own play sets the dial (Q5); the harness is a guard, not the judge |

## 8. Questions for the owner (one at a time; each with my recommendation)

1. **Do drives belong to each fighter or to the pair?** Per fighter: 3-4 slots each, and the orbit changes when you
   tag. Shared: one set that follows whoever is in play. *Recommendation: per fighter.* The tag becomes a build switch
   you can see, and Relay and Twin Soul only exist this way. The slice ships a knob so you can feel both.
2. **How are drives earned: floor drops in the fight, a pick of three between fights, or both?** *Recommendation:
   both.* Drops make power grow while you play; the pick is where you steer the build.
3. **A second copy of a drive levels it up (to 3), and there is no bag. Agreed?** *Recommendation: yes.* It keeps
   stacking and removes the inventory screens you called spam.
4. **When do keystones arrive?** *Recommendation: one at the run's start and one after each of the first two bosses,
   three per run.* A keystone at the start gives every run a shape from the first fight. The alternative is bosses
   only (two per run).
5. **How often should drives fire?** *Recommendation: start loud: about one fire every 2-3 seconds early and one a
   second with a full build, then trim after you have played it.* This is one dial; asked again after the slice.
6. **Which drive effects may launch a foe?** A launching burst can knock a foe out of your combo. *Recommendation:
   only the drives you time yourself (Nova's clock, Comet's landing, Spire's finisher) launch; drives that count your
   hits never do.*
7. **Do foes carry drives?** *Recommendation: elites and bosses carry one or two, orbiting them and named on their
   card; ordinary foes carry none.*
8. **In co-op, are floor drops per player or first touch?** *Recommendation: per player: each drop is marked with its
   owner's colour and only that player can take it.* First touch makes teammates race each other.
