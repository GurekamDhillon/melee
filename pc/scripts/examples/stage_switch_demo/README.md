Offline stage-switch demo. Select Final Destination, Battlefield, Yoshi's Story, Dream Land or Fountain of Dreams as the host, then enable this script mod before a match. The demo preloads all five stages and loops BF (wipe), Yoshi's Story (flash), Dream Land (morph), Fountain (wipe), FD (flash), after an initial FD wipe. Each queue entry waits ten logic seconds with a one-second indicator. The queue arms on the frame after the initial FD after-hook, once per match. A transient fighter-busy refusal keeps the same queued entry due and retries automatically; the rotation advances only after a successful completed transition. Console: `stage next`, `stage queue`, `stage slots`.

The default source path now destroys the outgoing native stage and runs the destination's complete retail init, load and start callbacks using a private working copy of cached FILE bytes. Yoshi's Story uses the actual Randall and Shy Guy controllers; Fountain uses the actual platform cycle and reflection camera. FD, BF, Yoshi's Story, Dream Land and Fountain are the supported source set. Pokemon Stadium, scrolling/transforming stages, target/1P stages, arbitrary DATs and mission sidecars are refused. Native game acceptance is still pending: source fixtures do not establish side-by-side visual equivalence.

Fighters retain damage, velocity and CPU control. Airborne fighters keep exact position and action unless outside a destination blast boundary, when they clamp one world unit inside it. Grounded fighters keep X and action and take the nearest destination floor within 512 world units above or below; without a floor they enter Fall at their current position. Ledge actions detach before destruction and then follow the airborne rule. A spawn is reserved for nonfinite coordinates or invalid bounds and is logged with its reason. Full retail switching uses this continuous rule even for the legacy KO placement option. Every fighter and subfighter loses old collision contacts. Portable held items and fighter projectiles keep their objects and positions while their collision contacts are cleared; items whose article or owner belongs to the outgoing stage are removed without death/explosion events. The match camera object survives and receives the destination's retail tilt, mode, bounds and clips. Destination fighter lights are rebuilt before the stage's on-load callback; fighter action/status overlays survive. Independent script geometry and seams survive; active or preloaded script enemies/targets refuse a switch because stage article registries conflict.

Save/load/rewind across a switch is refused. Offline same-epoch snapshot rules remain in force. Unloading reconstructs a fresh original retail visit; resource failure retains the current live visit instead of applying partial saved stage state.

Acceptance checklist (not yet executed): compare each supported destination with a normal match using the same inputs and RNG seed; watch Yoshi's Story for Randall's complete 1,200-frame lap and Shy Guys, Fountain through a complete platform cycle with its reflection, Dream Land through Whispy's wind cycle, and FD/BF through their background cycles. Check all ledges, blast zones, music, transitions during attacks/hitstun/ledge holds, Nana and six-player matches, held items and in-flight projectiles. Repeat switches and unload, inspect heap minima and confirm only one native stage remains live. Stadium transformations are refused rather than substituted.
